# Product Overview

**AgriShield** is an AI-powered autonomous crop risk intelligence platform for precision tomato farming. The system combines real-time field monitoring with autonomous rover-based intervention to detect and respond to agricultural threats before they impact yield.

## Core Capabilities

- **Multi-threat Detection**: Monitors pests (fruit borers, aphids), weeds (nightshade, pigweed), and climatic factors (humidity, heat stress, soil conditions)
- **Autonomous Rover Integration**: ESP32-based field rovers perform surveillance, sampling, and precision spray interventions (bio-pesticides, weedicides)
- **Real-time Risk Assessment**: Calculates field-level risk scores (LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL) with actionable farmer guidance
- **Multi-field Management**: Tracks multiple field polygons with crop-specific data (Roma, San Marzano, Beefsteak, Cherry tomatoes)

## User Experience

The platform presents an interactive cinematic dashboard with:
- **3-axis radial control** for navigating between Pests, Weeds, and Climatic alerts
- **Live field map visualization** with polygon overlays and risk indicators
- **Rover telemetry console** displaying GPS, battery, spray status, and sensor readings
- **Scene-based alert cards** with bounding box detection overlays, confidence scores, and intervention timelines
- **Scenario simulation** allowing users to switch between different field conditions

## Target Users

- Commercial tomato farmers managing 30-65 acre plots
- Agricultural research institutions studying Solanaceae crop management
- Farm managers requiring early intervention for pest/disease outbreaks
